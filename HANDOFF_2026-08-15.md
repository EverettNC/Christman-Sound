## The ear (Corti) — current contract

Per-utterance card: `kind`, `duration`, `attack`, `decay`, `peak RMS`,
`median F0` (or null), `last F1/F2`, `mean ZCR`, `voiced`. Ring of 8.

Plus `tape: TapeFrame[]`, cap 360:
`{ t: ms from onset, rms, zcr, f0: number | null }`

- **The cap drops the TAIL.** Past ~6s the ending is gone — every
  end-of-utterance feature must be `None` when `truncated`.
- **`t` is `performance.now()`, ~16ms per animation frame, NOT uniform.** Slopes
  must use real `t`. A gap in `t` is a dropped frame, not a pause.
- **Null `f0` is a hole, not a zero.** Never interpolate across it.
- Jitter/shimmer/HNR are per-frame only and die with the frame.
- **Cold start: no speaker, no baseline.** `kind` is not load-bearing.
- **The ear measures. It does not classify.** Do not wire Corti as
  `SoundDetectorBackend` and do not map `grunt → distress` — that is the same
  lie with a new name.
